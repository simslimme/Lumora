Privacy Policy

Last updated:August 2026

Lumora Voices ("we", "this site") is an independent review website. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts and do not ask for personal information to browse this site. We collect two kinds of data:

Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site — pages visited, referral sources, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, scroll depth, and which links and buttons are clicked. Microsoft Clarity also records anonymized session replays and heatmaps showing mouse movement, clicks and scrolling, which we use to improve page layout and usability. These tools set cookies and use similar technologies, and process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies (Google, Microsoft). We do not use them to identify individual visitors, and we do not sell this data.

Email addresses (optional). If you subscribe to our newsletter or download a resource, we collect the email address you provide. We use it only to send the content you requested and occasional related updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from our list.

Affiliate links and third parties

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, the destination site (for example, ElevenLabs or its affiliate network) may set cookies in your browser to attribute a potential purchase to this site. We do not control those cookies and do not receive personal information about you from them — we see only aggregate, anonymized statistics such as click and conversion counts. If you purchase through an affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics, set by Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity as described above. We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies. Third-party services you click through to — such as affiliate networks — may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. You can block or clear cookies at any time in your browser settings, use your browser's Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control signal, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. If you sign up for emails, your email address is stored by Kit (kit.com), the service we use to send them. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

How we protect and retain data

Email addresses are stored with our email service provider and retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.Analytics data is retained according to our providers' standard retention periods (Google Analytics: up to 14 months; Microsoft Clarity: up to 30 days for session recordings) and is used in aggregate.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you (in practice, this is your email address if you subscribed). Contact us and we will action the request promptly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves; the date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be noted on this page.
