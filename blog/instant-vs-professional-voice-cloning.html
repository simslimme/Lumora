Instant vs Professional Voice Cloning: Which ElevenLabs Plan Do You Need?

ElevenLabs has two ways to clone your voice. Instant Voice Cloning works from a short recording, with no training wait. Professional Voice Cloning trains a dedicated model on much more audio and sounds closer to you, but it takes hours and needs a higher plan. Here's which one you need, and which plan gets it.

The short answer

  • Pick Starter (Instant Voice Cloning) if you want to try cloning your voice, make short videos or social clips, or you're on a tight budget.
  • Pick Creator (Professional Voice Cloning) if your voice is the product (a YouTube channel, podcast, course or audiobook), or if Instant cloning doesn't capture your accent.

Which one for what you're making

  • YouTube voiceovers or a faceless channel: start with Instant on Starter. Move to Professional once you post every week and viewers hear your voice a lot.
  • Podcasts, courses and audiobooks: Professional. Over long listening, small differences become obvious.
  • Dubbing into other languages: either works. ElevenLabs says Eleven v4 keeps your voice with a native accent in other languages. See our Swahili dubbing guide.
  • An uncommon accent: ElevenLabs says Instant cloning may struggle with uncommon accents and suggests Professional if it doesn't capture yours.
  • Just curious: Starter's first month costs $1.

Before you upgrade

  • Record clean audio first. A bad recording makes both kinds of clone worse. See our guide on how much audio you need.
  • Professional cloning needs at least 30 minutes of clean audio with only you speaking, so plan a recording session.
  • Only clone your own voice, or one you have permission to use.
  • For monetised videos or client work you need a paid plan, which includes the commercial license.

FAQ

Can I clone my voice for free?

No. The Free plan doesn't include voice cloning. Starter is the cheapest plan that does.

How much audio do I need?

Instant: at least 10 seconds, with 1–2 minutes recommended. Professional: at least 30 minutes, with 2–3 hours recommended.

How long does a Professional clone take?

Several hours of training. ElevenLabs says roughly 3–6 hours.

Can I start on Starter and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Instant cloning and move to Creator when you need a Professional voice.

Can I use my clone commercially?

Yes, on any paid plan, which includes a commercial license.
